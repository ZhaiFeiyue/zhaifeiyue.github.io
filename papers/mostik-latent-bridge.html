Latent Communication Between AI Models — L3 (per-paper synthesis) #

Target: mostik-latent-bridge (Mostik.ai vendor blog, 2026-09-11). A frozen-endpoints learned "bridge" that translates one model's hidden states into another's, letting a 4B model consume a 753B model's latent reasoning instead of its text. Positioned here against 8 KB peers spanning the substrate that makes latent transfer plausible, the text-based collaboration baselines it attacks, and the compression/security results that constrain its claims.

1. 相关论文 #

The 8 peers are not a topical cluster — they are the **support, baseline, and

counter-evidence* for a single thesis: text is a lossy interface between

models, and the fix is to move reasoning in latent form*

[mostik-latent-bridge]. They sort into five roles:

A. 潜在表示可转移的"底物" (why latent could carry reasoning).

  • Transformer (1706.03762) is the substrate itself: every "hidden state"

    • the bridge would transmit is the per-layer output of stacked multi-head

    attention [1706.03762]. The blog's grounding claim — that

    hidden states hold unspoken reasoning (J-space / Jacobian lens) — is a claim

    about the internals of this architecture.

  • DreamerV3 (2301.04104) is the strongest existing proof-of-concept that a

    • system can train and act entirely inside a learned latent space: its

    actor-critic never touches the real environment, learning only on the world

    model's imagined latent trajectories [2301.04104]. This is

    the closest published precedent for "compute in latent, not in observations."

B. 模型间协作的现行接口 = text (the baseline being attacked).

  • RAG (2005.11401) chains a retriever and a generator through a *text/

    • passage* interface, marginalizing a retrieved document as a latent variable

    [2005.11401]. It is the canonical "augment one model with an

    external source" pipeline the blog wants to replace with latent handoff.

  • Verifiers (2110.14168) is a two-network pipeline (generator → verifier)

    • that communicates through sampled text solutions [2110.14168];

    the blog's "councils / advisor / escalation" use-case is exactly this shape.

  • RAPTOR (2401.18059) combines evidence across a document via multi-level

    • text summaries [2401.18059] — an abstraction-level

    alternative to latent fusion.

C. teacher→student (the distillation use-case).

  • Llama 3 (2407.21783) aligns via rejection sampling + SFT + DPO, i.e.

    • teacher supervision applied post-hoc on sampled text

    [2407.21783]. The blog's headline future direction —

    teachers supervising students during generation — is a direct contrast to

    this sampled-text distillation loop.

D. 压缩约束 (the number that undermines the premise).

  • SageAttention (2410.02367) quantizes attention activations to INT8 with

    • <0.2% end-to-end quality loss [2410.02367], a direct

    empirical statement about how much of those "~2 MB of internal numbers" is

    actually load-bearing.

E. 安全攻击面 (what a latent channel removes).

  • StruQ (2402.06363) shows that model-collaboration safety comes from

    • separating and filtering a text channel [2402.06363];

    a latent channel has no analogous human-auditable boundary.

2. 本篇 vs 相关论文的 delta #

What is genuinely new. The novel object is a *trained translation layer

between two independently-pretrained representation geometries, with both

endpoints frozen* [mostik-latent-bridge]. None of the 8 peers do this:

AxisPeer baselinemostik-latent-bridge delta
Inter-model interfacetext/passage (RAG, Verifiers, RAPTOR)raw hidden state via bridge
Trainable surfacequery encoder only, frozen doc index (RAG)both endpoints frozen, only bridge trained
Latent space scopeone model's own latent (DreamerV3)cross-model, heterogeneous width/tokenizer
Teacher supervisionpost-hoc on sampled text (Llama 3)in-generation latent (proposed)
  • vs RAG. RAG already treats collaboration as "inject external content and

    • marginalize," and already proved you can freeze one side (the document

    encoder + index) and train only the query encoder to rotate onto a static

    manifold [2005.11401]. mostik-latent-bridge's delta — and its risk — is

    that it freezes both sides and aligns via a third module. RAG's own result

    suggests at least one side usually has to move; a both-frozen bridge is a

    strictly harder alignment problem than anything RAG demonstrated.

  • vs DreamerV3. Dreamer is the delta's best friend and its sharpest

    • qualifier. It proves latent-space computation works — but only *within a

    single model's own* RSSM latent, learned jointly with the decoder

    [2301.04104]. mostik-latent-bridge needs the same magic *across two

    networks that never co-trained*. The entire unproven leap is "representation

    geometries of GLM-5.2 753B and Qwen-3.5 4B are alignable by a learned map,"

    which Dreamer neither claims nor tests.

  • vs Llama 3 distillation. Llama 3's teacher signal is sampled text, scored

    • by a reward model [2407.21783]; mostik-latent-bridge proposes latent

    supervision during decoding. This is a real conceptual advance if the

    cross-model bridge exists — but mostik-latent-bridge supplies none of the machinery

    (loss, data, alignment procedure) that Llama 3 documents in full.

  • Contradiction with SageAttention. mostik-latent-bridge's load-bearing framing is that

    • one token materializes ~1M activations (~2 MB) of which only ~17 bits survive

    as text — a ~$10^6\times$ compression that "discards nearly all" the reasoning

    [mostik-latent-bridge]. SageAttention measures the opposite: those same

    attention activations tolerate INT8 quantization at <0.2% end-to-end loss,

    i.e. most of the 2 MB is redundant low-entropy content, not precious reasoning

    [2410.02367].

矛盾根源: the two are measuring different quantities. mostik-latent-bridge counts the

raw bit-width of the activation tensor (2 MB) and contrasts it with the

symbol entropy of one emitted token (~17 bits); SageAttention measures the

task-relevant information that survives aggressive quantization (Table 1 /

Table 8, <0.2% metric loss) [2410.02367]. Both numbers are

correct, but mostik-latent-bridge's rhetorical "~$10^6\times$ of discarded reasoning"

conflates tensor size with usable information — SageAttention is direct

evidence that the usable fraction is small, so the upper bound on what any

bridge can recover is far below the headline framing.

3. 可攻击面 #

Direct rebuttals to specific claims, each anchored in a peer or the self-L2:

  1. "2 MB of reasoning is thrown away" overstates the prize. If activations

    2. survive INT8 quantization with <0.2% quality loss

    [2410.02367], the information a bridge could usefully

    transfer is bounded well below 2 MB. The compression framing is marketing,

    not an information-theoretic bound.

  2. No benchmark, baseline, or error bar. Every performance figure (+25%, 2×,

    3. 2.5× compute, +10 pts) is vendor-reported with no named task

    [mostik-latent-bridge]. Contrast the peer bar: Llama 3

    reports MMLU/HumanEval/GSM8K/MATH against GPT-4 with confidence intervals

    [2407.21783]; SageAttention reports per-model CosSim and

    end-to-end deltas [2410.02367].

  3. Both-frozen cross-model alignment is the hard part, and it is undisclosed.

    4. The only reproducible analog — RAG's frozen document side — still *trains the

    query encoder* to match a static manifold [2005.11401].

    mostik-latent-bridge freezes both endpoints and hides the bridge mechanics entirely; the

    claim rests precisely on the one piece nobody can inspect.

  4. More "signal" is not monotonically better. Verifiers shows that injecting

    5. more search/supervision into a small model can hurt: test@N coverage

    collapses as the generator sharpens, and top-1 accuracy is non-monotone in

    sample count (rises then falls past ~400) [2110.14168].

    A bridge that floods a 4B model with a 753B model's full latent could

    similarly collapse the small model's own solution diversity — a failure mode

    the blog never addresses.

  5. The latent channel deletes a safety boundary. StruQ's entire defense is

    6. that a text channel can be delimited and recursively filtered so injected

    control tokens cannot survive [2402.06363]. A hidden-state

    channel is unfilterable by construction: there is no "reserved token" to

    strip, no human-auditable payload. Replacing text handoffs with latent

    handoffs in "councils / escalation systems" trades an auditable interface for

    an opaque one — a security regression the blog markets as a feature.

    4. 生态位 #

    Paradigm positioning. For a decade the universal inter-model interface has

    been text: RAG passes passages [2005.11401], Verifiers

    passes sampled solutions [2110.14168], RAPTOR passes summaries

    [2401.18059], Llama 3 distills from sampled generations

    [2407.21783]. mostik-latent-bridge's bet is that this text interface is

    the bottleneck, and that a latent handoff is a new primitive sitting between

    distillation and multi-agent orchestration. That is a genuine paradigm-shift

    framing — if the primitive is real.

    Where the plausibility comes from. The niche is credible only because two

    upstream results exist: the Transformer's hidden states demonstrably encode

    anticipatory structure (the J-space / interpretability grounding)

    [1706.03762], and DreamerV3 proves an agent can compute

    usefully inside a learned latent space [2301.04104].

    mostik-latent-bridge is riding the plausibility these establish, without clearing their

    evidentiary bar.

    Adoption evidence — effectively none. The honest contrast is with what

    "real" looks like in this KB. SageAttention ships open, `pip install

    sageattention`, integrated into ComfyUI/Diffusers and adopted by follow-on work

    [2410.02367]; RAG's reference implementation lives

    in HuggingFace Transformers [2005.11401]; Llama 3's

    weights are public [2407.21783]. mostik-latent-bridge has no

    code, no benchmark, an anonymous byline, and a 4-month-old company

    [mostik-latent-bridge]. Its ecological niche today is *press

    narrative*, not deployed primitive.

    5. 未探索方向 #

    Hybrid and adaptive directions the cluster suggests — the cheap, testable

    science the blog skips:

    1. Cheapest falsification first (self-advice). Before any proprietary

      2. bridge, compare text relay vs logit/embedding relay on your own

      router→expert / draft→verify / council pipeline [mostik-latent-bridge].

      This isolates "is text actually the bottleneck?" with no bridge at all.

    2. Quantify the actually-transferable bits. Resolve the §2 contradiction

      3. empirically: sweep latent precision à la SageAttention

      [2410.02367] and measure how many bits of hidden state

      must cross before the downstream accuracy gain saturates. This converts

      "~$10^6\times$ discarded" from rhetoric into a measured compression curve.

    3. Latent verifier (bridge × Verifiers). Instead of a verifier re-reading

      4. sampled text, let a token-level value head read the generator's hidden state

      directly [2110.14168] — a latent analog that, if it works,

      would also be the first independent test of the cross-model-readout premise.

    4. Tree-of-latents (bridge × RAPTOR). RAPTOR fuses multi-level text

      5. summaries [2401.18059]; the latent analog is a hierarchy of

      bridged summary-states at multiple abstraction levels, adaptively selected

      per query.

    5. In-generation latent distillation (bridge × Llama 3). Replace post-hoc

      6. rejection sampling [2407.21783] with a teacher that nudges

      the student's hidden state during decoding — the blog's own stated direction,

      but framed as a controlled ablation against the sampled-text baseline Llama 3

      already quantifies.

    6. Alignment-first, not performance-first. The open research object is the

      7. map between two independently-pretrained geometries (RAG aligns only

      query-side to a static index [2005.11401]; Dreamer aligns

      only within one model [2301.04104]). A reproducible study of

      when two frozen Transformer representation spaces admit a low-rank bridge —

      and when they don't — is the missing science the vendor claim presumes.
